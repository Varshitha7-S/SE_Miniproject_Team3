\- **Requirement:** HMS-NFR-001

\- **Executed by:** Varshitha S (QA Lead)

\- **Execution Date:** 31 October 2026

\- **Environment:** Windows desktop, Google Chrome

\- **Participants:** Five representative demo users

## 2. Test Objectives

The objective of this usability evaluation was to assess whether representative users could independently locate student information and check room availability using the Hostel Management System.

The evaluation focused on the clarity of labels, ease of navigation, task completion, and the ability of users to operate the application without assistance.

## 3. Test Procedure

Each participant was independently asked to perform the following tasks:

**Task 1 - Student Search**

Find student STU003 using the student search control and identify the student's name.

**Expected Result:** The participant should successfully locate STU003 and identify the correct student, Rohan Kapoor.

**Task 2 - Room Availability**

Find a room that currently has available space and report its room number and available capacity.

**Expected Result:** The participant should successfully identify a room with available capacity and report its available spaces correctly.

**Testing Conditions:**

1\. Each participant performed the tasks independently.

2\. No verbal guidance or hints were provided during task execution.

3\. Participant identities were anonymized.

4\. Task completion and assistance requirements were observed.
